About the Team

Independent Technology & Game Development Collective

The Citadel

Technology should empower people, not make them dependent.

The Citadel is an independent technology and game development collective focused on building applications, software, digital systems and videogames.

We believe technology should be a tool for independence, creativity and progress. Our purpose is to develop solutions that give people greater control over the tools they use, the systems they rely on and the digital environments they inhabit.

We are not simply interested in building technology. We are interested in building technology that matters.

Our purpose

Purpose // Independence

Building towards independence.

Technology has become an essential part of everyday life. Yet much of it depends on centralized platforms, external services and decisions made beyond the reach of its users.

We believe people and organizations should have greater freedom in choosing how they use and interact with technology.

Our mission is to create useful tools, software and digital experiences that encourage technological independence, preserve user control and provide practical solutions to real-world problems.

We do not believe independence requires rejecting existing technologies. We believe it requires understanding them, choosing them deliberately and maintaining control over the things we build.

Our philosophy

Philosophy // Pragmatic Independence

Pragmatic Independence. Independence through choice, not isolation.

We do not reject commercial platforms, proprietary software or external infrastructures simply because they are not our own. We recognize their value and use them when they provide the right tools for the job.

However, we believe technological decisions should always be deliberate. We aim to avoid unnecessary dependencies, preserve control over our work and maintain the ability to adapt when circumstances change.

We value interoperability, maintainability, portability and technical knowledge because they allow projects to evolve beyond their original environments.

Our independence is not defined by what we refuse to use, but by our ability to choose what we use.

Continuity

Principle // Continuity

A bastion dedicated to Continuity.

Continuity is the principle that defines The Citadel. To us, continuity means preserving the ability to create, maintain and use technology regardless of changes in external circumstances.

Software should not become useless simply because a service disappears. Knowledge should not be lost when a project ends. Digital tools should have the possibility to evolve beyond the environments in which they were originally created.

We aim to reduce unnecessary dependencies, preserve technical knowledge and build solutions that can continue to exist and evolve over time.

We understand that complete independence is not always practical or desirable. What matters is maintaining awareness, control and the ability to adapt.

Continuity is not just about keeping things alive. It is about preserving the ability to move forward.

Our manifesto

Principles worth building upon.

Principle 01

Independence through choice

We choose technologies based on their usefulness, reliability and suitability. We do not confuse independence with isolation.

Principle 02

Technology must serve a purpose

We build to solve problems, create opportunities and provide useful experiences. Technology is a means, not an end in itself.

Principle 03

Adaptability is a strength

Tools, platforms and methods change. We preserve the ability to learn, adapt and evolve rather than tying our future to a single solution.

Principle 04

Knowledge must endure

We value documentation, maintainability and the preservation of technical knowledge. What we learn should become a foundation for future work.

Principle 05

Human control matters

Technology should remain a tool in human hands. Decisions, responsibilities and meaningful control should not disappear behind automated systems.

Principle 06

Build for continuity

Every project contributes to a larger body of knowledge and experience. We aim to create things that can be maintained, improved and carried forward.

How we operate

Structure // Flexible

One identity. Different initiatives.

The Citadel operates as a flexible collective rather than a rigid organization.

Our initiatives can develop independently, collaborate with one another or bring together external contributors depending on the nature of each project.

Our work includes commissioned development, independent productions, technological experimentation and long-term internal projects.

We adapt our structure, teams and tools to the challenges we undertake. The structure may change. The identity and principles remain.

Our vision

Vision // Beyond the present

Building beyond the present.

The Citadel is not limited to the projects we develop today. We aim to explore new possibilities in independent computing, software development, digital infrastructure, artificial intelligence and interactive entertainment.

Our long-term ambition is to develop an increasingly capable technological ecosystem: one that combines external tools, independent solutions and internally developed technologies according to practical needs.

Some of these ambitions are already taking shape. Others remain open challenges that we intend to explore.

We see every project as an opportunity to acquire knowledge, develop new capabilities and build the foundations for what comes next.

Build with us

Collaborate

The Citadel welcomes meaningful projects, collaborations and new ideas.

Whether you need a technological solution, want to develop a project or are interested in collaborating with our collective, we welcome the opportunity to build something together.
