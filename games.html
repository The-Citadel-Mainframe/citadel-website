Escape Subsector 086

Released

Escape Subsector 086 is an ASCII endless runner set in a Citadel subsector in containment failure. Run through the ruins, jump and slide past the obstacles, and parry the hostile robots and beasts before they strike. The longer you survive, the faster the run gets.

  • Jump, slide and parry: three moves, no second chances
  • Speed and enemies escalate with your score
  • Your personal best is saved on your device
  • Keyboard and touch controls
  • Soundtrack: “kill TIME” by Subsector086Compiler
