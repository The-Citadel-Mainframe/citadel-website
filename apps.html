NAO — Nike Adapt Open

Completed

NAO is a free, open-source Android app that lets you keep using your Nike Adapt BB 2.0 shoes after the official app was discontinued. Connect the left and right shoe, tighten or loosen the laces, save lace presets, control the lights and check the battery.

Platform
Android
Developer
Citadel — App Developments
Status
Completed
Distribution
GitHub only (build from source)
Not available on any app store. NAO is not published on Google Play or elsewhere. To use it, download the source code from GitHub and compile it yourself.

Dual Brothers App

Active development

Dual Brothers App is the official app of Dual Brothers Tech, a workshop dedicated to the repair, assistance and maintenance of computer devices, smartphones and hardware in general. The workshop commissioned the app, which is developed by Citadel.

The app runs diagnostics on the user's phone and records the results. The user can turn them into a WhatsApp message and send it directly to the shop, so the assistance they receive is targeted from the first contact.
